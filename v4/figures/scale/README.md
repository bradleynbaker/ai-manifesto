# F3 · The spend steelyard

A steelyard on a heavy bench base. The beam is graduated on a log scale of annual AI spend per employee, $100 → $1M (one major tick per ×10, printed $100, $1K, $10K, $100K, $1M), with a long tick and wedge at $250K. The black poise (the one accent) slides along the beam to the spend. A fixed counterweight stamped **$250K**, Jensen Huang's bar, hangs plumb from the short arm. Below the bar the beam tips toward the counterweight: the tongue at the pivot leans off **LEVEL**, and the beam end rides up in its trig loop, well above the loop's level line. At $250K the beam levels.

- **Slide title:** "The median business spends $137 a year per person on AI; the best spend half a salary."
- **Truth:** `model.mjs`. Ramp AI Index, June 2026 (as reported), 70,000+ US businesses, monthly × 12: median $11.38/mo → $137/yr; 90th percentile $611/mo → $7,332/yr; top 1% $7,449/mo → $89,388/yr (growing ~14% a month). Huang (Tom's Hardware): "if that $500,000 engineer did not consume at least $250,000 worth of tokens, I'm going to be deeply alarmed": $250,000/yr, half a $500K salary. The spend between states is interpolated on a log scale. **The tilt is illustrative** (6° at the median, √ of the decades short of the bar, level at the bar), and the caption says so.
- **States** (`window.figureApi.states`): `median` ($137, 0.03%), `90th pct` ($7,332, 1.5%), `top 1%` ($89,388, 18%), `Jensen` ($250,000, 50%, beam level). The readout shows the label and $/yr. The `.board` shows PERCENTILE | SPEND / YEAR | SHARE OF $500K SALARY.
- **Key state:** `median` (state 0). The gap is the point. `scale.svg` and `scale.png` show it.
- **Build:** `node build.mjs` writes `scale.html` (standalone, paper theme), `scale.fragment.html`, `scale.svg` (key state) and `scale-1.svg` … `scale-4.svg` (median, 90th pct, top 1%, Jensen), each a static SVG of that state built without JS. The poise's ink is inlined in every SVG.
- **Code:** `geom.mjs` holds the moving parts (the tilting beam with its scale, the poise, the tongue, the hanger offset). It is used by the build and inlined into the page, so the static and live drawings are the same code. Tilted solids are convex prisms rotated about the pivot axis and shaded in the kit's four tones (`sySolid`), and face text is laid in the rotated beam plane.
- **Live:** a calm idle tour (2.8 s per state); pointer-across or arrow keys steer it; it stops offscreen and holds still under reduced motion. `figureApi.set(i)` eases to a state with no idle resume, and `hold()` stops the tour. The embed flags come from `_anatomy/embed.mjs`.
- **Checks:** line-end check `0 of 116 ends float` in all four states, three runs each (`drive.mjs` `["lines"]`), plus `lines.mjs scale.html`. Desktop and embed (1280×720) captured. Phone (390 px): no sideways scroll, and the board stacks. Reduced motion captured. 4× close-ups taken of the pivot and dial fan, the poise, the trig loop and the hanger/base. Smallest face text is 5.8 world units at scale 1.32 (the floor is 4.0, equivalent to 3.6 at the token meter's 1.48).
- **Stage:** `_anatomy/stage.mjs`. Own CSS adds only a three-column board and the poise's dark ink.
- **Size:** Figure, about 60 parts, no shader.
