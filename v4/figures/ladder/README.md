# Fig 8 · The sovereign ladder

A fenced yard on a ground plate. Inside the fence, three stepped rungs you own rise left to right, each with its hardware drawn small but real: **device** (desks with NPU laptops and a desktop — fills with this 2026–27 refresh), **workgroup** (a desktop inference box on a team bench and a half rack), **core** (a short rack row in a cage). A router at the foot sends request tokens up the rungs one at a time; each token carries a small card, its sensitive context. Most dock in a rung's sockets. Outside the fence, the one opening is the **gate** — a turnstile and a meter on the pillar (the figure's only red), then a press over the deck whose cabinet reads SENSITIVE CONTEXT STRIPPED — leading to a much taller tower: **FRONTIER**, metered.

- **Truth:** `model.mjs`. Artificial Analysis's current comparison page, Opus 5 vs Nemotron 3 Ultra: FRONTIER 61 on the intelligence index · $3.85/M · 47 tok/s (outside, metered) against US OPEN 38 · $0.58/M · 100 tok/s (inside, uncapped). "Smarter by a third. Nearly seven times the price. Half the speed." No other numbers are used. The token mix (30 a cycle: 9 per rung, 3 through the gate), spacing and speed are illustrative choreography, and the caption says so.
- **Printed on the object:** rung names on the rung faces (DEVICE · 2026–27, WORKGROUP, CORE), ROUTER, GATE, the press cabinet's SENSITIVE / CONTEXT / STRIPPED, the plinth under the yard (US OPEN 38 · $0.58/M · 100 TOK/S · UNCAPPED) and the tower's sign (FRONTIER · 61 · $3.85/M · 47 TOK/S · METERED). Smallest face text 6.6 units = 11.5 px on a 1200 px page (the token meter's 3.6-unit floor is 11.2 px); the build logs it.
- **States** (`window.figureApi.states`): `device`, `workgroup`, `core`, `gate`, `all`. Each eases the tokens of the rungs in play in and the rest out, raises that rung's label and sockets, and names it in the readout. `gate` dims the rungs and, if no escalation is due within a few seconds, blinks the tokens and picks the flow up 2.4 s before the next pair reaches the press, so a click always shows the card being stamped off, the turnstile turning and the meter clicking.
- **Key state:** `all` (index 4) — the full ladder, many tokens on the rungs, a few through the gate → `ladder.svg`. Per-state static snapshots (no JS): `ladder-1.svg` … `ladder-5.svg` in state order; the gate snapshot freezes one token under the press with its card and one stripped past it.
- **Board:** RUNG | MODEL INDEX | $ / M TOKENS | TOK / S | METERED?, two rows (the rungs, the frontier); the row not in play fades. Note line: the three-sentence comparison.
- **Build:** `node build.mjs` → `ladder.html` (standalone, paper theme, `PAPER_CSS + STAGE_CSS + EMBED_CSS` plus a small board layout of its own), `ladder.svg`, `ladder-1..5.svg`. `ladder.png` is the 1200 px desktop preview of the key state.
- **Embed:** `ladder.html?embed=1&state=N&still=1&board=0` as for every v4 figure.
- **Checks:** line-end check 0 floating ends in every state and three runs in a row on the live page (Anatomy `scripts/lines.mjs`); desktop 1200, phone 390 (no sideways scroll), hover, the idle tour, reduced motion, the embed at 1600×900, and 3–4× close-ups of the gate, rungs and tower captured and read. The SVG snapshots parse as XML.
- **Size:** Hero: about 170 static solids (fence posts and rails, feet and small desk legs make up a third of them) plus 30 moving tokens; no shader.
