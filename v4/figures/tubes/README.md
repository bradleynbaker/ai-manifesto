# F11 · The chain of command (`tubes`)

A pneumatic-tube message system on a stepped stand. The AI lead's desk sits at the front left, with a send console, a lever, a capsule rack and one red capsule in the cradle. A riser climbs to a diverter manifold, and its shuttle slides in a slot to pick one of three tubes. The tubes run up the steps to three offices, each one step higher: CFO, CIO, CEO. Every office has a receiver, a bin and a card holder. When you send, the shuttle switches, the lever is pulled and the capsule (the only accent, in red) rides the chosen tube into that office's receiver and drops into the bin. Then the office's card rises with the one sentence it can sign.

- **Slide it proves:** "Selling up the chain means sending each office the one sentence it can sign."
- **Truth:** `model.mjs` holds the manifesto's wording and no numbers. Each card is the office's sentence cut to two lines: CFO "A CAP COSTS MORE / THAN CAPACITY", CIO "IT RULES / FOR MODELS", CEO "OUR ASSET OR / THEIR TRAINING DATA". The board's note is the longer sentence the speaker says. The drivers follow the talk's order: pricing → CFO, security → CIO, operations → CEO. The machine itself is illustrative, and the caption says so.
- **States** (`window.figureApi.states`): `CFO`, `CIO`, `CEO`, `all three`. Moving to one office empties the bins, lowers the cards and brings the capsule home. Then the diverter switches, the lever is pulled, the capsule travels and drops into the bin, and the card rises. "All three" raises all three cards in turn and leaves the capsule in the cradle.
- **Key state:** `all three` (index 3), with every card up. It is written to `tubes.svg` and is also the page's first frame. Static snapshots for each state are `tubes-1.svg` (CFO) through `tubes-4.svg` (all three).
- **Readout:** the office. **Board:** OFFICE · DRIVER · ON THE CARD, plus the speaker's sentence.
- **Interaction:** pointing at an office (with a 320 ms dwell) or clicking sends to it, and pointing at the desk selects all three. Arrow keys, Home/End and 1–4 step through the states. An idle tour pauses about 2.6 s on each state. Reduced motion jumps straight to each state. In the deck, `?embed=1&state=N&still=1` snaps to state N at load.
- **Type:** card text is 7.2 units (the 19-character CEO line fills the card) and the office nameplates are 8 units. The diverter's outlet labels are 4.4 units and sit beside the shuttle's path, never under it.
- **Build:** `node build.mjs` writes `tubes.html`, `tubes.svg` and `tubes-1..4.svg`. The capsule is hidden while it is inside or behind the diverter box, the console or a receiver. This is worked out at build time by ray-casting each route sample against those boxes, and the page only draws the visible runs.
- **Vendored:** `vendor/lathe.mjs` and `vendor/tube.mjs` are copied from the Anatomy skill, with their import changed to `../../_anatomy/kit/iso-kit.mjs`. They are used at build time only, for the tubes, flanges, clamps, couplings and bell mouth. `_anatomy/` itself is untouched.
- **Checks:** `lines.mjs` reported 0 floating ends on three runs in a row and in every still state (0–3). Desktop, contact sheet of a CIO send, 4× close-ups (bin, cradle, diverter, card), phone at 390 px (no sideways scroll), reduced motion and embed captures were all taken, with a clean console.
- **Size:** Figure, about 75 parts, no shader.
