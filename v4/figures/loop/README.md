# F1 · The leak loop

A conveyor loop on a bench. A black puck, the user's intent and the figure's only accent, rides the chain past seven stations: **1 USAGE EXPLODES** (a pin board whose pins shoot up), **2 THE METER ARRIVES** (a meter stepping through four stops into an overflow wedge), **3 CAP THE NATIVES** (a screw clamp that presses down on the puck, plus the points lever), **4 GO PERSONAL** (a cabinet whose hatch opens on a phone), **5 PERSONAL ACCOUNTS TRAIN** (a recording drum with its pen and the training toggle), **6 INTENT → ROADMAP** (a 30-compartment sorting tray that fills into a density map), and **7 THE SPIKE EATS THE 80%** (a press driving a spike into the dense middle of a map). At station 3 a points switch can shunt the puck off the loop into the fenced pen **8 OWNED / OWN THE BOUNDARY**, so the loop never reaches 4.

- **Truth:** `model.mjs` holds only the spec's numbers and words: 0 → 19,000 users in 45 days (AI Weekly, Aug 2026); 66% / 88% shadow AI (PagerDuty, June 2026, n = 1,250); training toggle and 5-year retention (Anthropic consumer terms, Aug 2025); 30+ categories of work (Anthropic Economic Index). The machine is illustrative, and the caption says so.
- **Geometry and drawing:** `parts.mjs` is shared by the build (static SVGs) and the live page, where it is inlined in a closure. Every moving part is a pure function of one pose. The puck is re-slotted between the pen's back fences and its front kerb by true depth.
- **States** (`window.figureApi.states`): `1`…`7` put the puck at that station and play its action. `8 · break` throws the switch (the lever at 3 swings, the blade crosses the channel), sends the puck into OWNED, and turns 4–7 faint (`data-dead` token remap). The readout shows `Step N · NAME`, and the scoreboard shows the step, the station, the number to say out loud and the evidence sentence.
- **Key state:** step 4 (`loop.svg`, `loop.png`). This is the full loop, with intent already leaking out of the side hatch. Per-state snapshots are `loop-1.svg` … `loop-8.svg` (`loop-8.svg` is the break).
- **Motion:** an idle tour of about 3.3 s per state (4.6 s on the break). Pointing at a station steers the puck to it; ← → or 1–8 step through the states. The puck waits for the clamp to lift and for the blade to finish its throw. Reduced motion jumps between states, and the loop stops when offscreen.
- **Build:** `node build.mjs` → `loop.html` (standalone, paper theme, with board and embed support) and the nine SVGs.
- **Checks:** `lines.mjs` reports 0 floating ends in all 8 states, and 3 runs in a row on the key state. All SVGs pass `xmllint`. Desktop, phone (390 px, no sideways scroll), reduced motion, a 3 → break transition sheet and a 16-frame idle-tour sheet were captured and looked at, and the console is clean.
- **Size:** Hero, about 170 solids in the key state, no shader. Face text is 6 units at scale 0.90, which is the token meter's 3.6-unit floor (5.9 units at this scale). Numerals are 13.5 units.
