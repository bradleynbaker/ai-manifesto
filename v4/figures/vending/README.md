# Fig 7 · One token, four prices (`vending`)

A vending machine that sells one item, **1M TOKENS · SAME OPEN MODEL**, through four coin slots with price plates: OWNED CAPACITY, CLOUD GPU $0.97, FRONTIER API ~$12, WRAPPED FEDERAL LLM ≤$45. The owned slot's plate is a red drum counter (the figure's one accent), driven by a square shaft from the crank box beside the machine. The crank box has a 24-hour dial labelled HOURS / DAY · THE BOX IS BUSY. Its shaded wedge is the busy part of the day, and a notch marks 5.3 h BREAK-EVEN. Turn the crank and the drums roll to the owned price.

- **Slide:** "Past 5.3 hours a day, owning the box beats renting it — and the natives get you there."
- **Truth (`model.mjs`):** owned $/M = 0.97 × 5.3 / hours (the manifesto's Figure 9, Lenovo 2026 TCO, vendor study: 5-year lifecycle, zero residual, 12% maintenance, $0.12/kWh). That gives 1 h $5.14 · 3 h $1.71 · 5.3 h $0.97 (parity with cloud GPU) · 12 h $0.43 · 24 h $0.21, and about a 5.2-month break-even for an 8-GPU system against on-demand cloud. The frontier rate (~$12/M, blended coding) and wrapped federal rate (up to $45/M) are Brad's reads from list pricing; the caption says so.
- **Verdict (board note):** ≤ $0.97 "Owned capacity wins — this is your natives' duty cycle." · ≤ $12 "Owned already beats the frontier API blended rate." · otherwise "Renting wins — until the natives show up." Because 1 h already gives $5.14, the third line never shows in the 1–24 h range.
- **States (`figureApi.states`):** `1 h`, `3 h`, `5.3 h · break-even`, `12 h · a native's day`.
- **Key state:** `12 h · a native's day` (index 3). It is in `vending.svg` and `vending.png`, and the page starts there. Per-state static SVGs are `vending-1.svg` … `vending-4.svg`, built at build time with the dial, crank and drums already set.
- **Controls:** pointer across the figure turns the crank continuously from 1 to 24 h. Arrow keys move ±1 h (Shift for ±0.1 h), Home/End go to 1/24 h, PageUp/PageDown step through the states, and keys 1–4 jump to a state. When idle, it tours 1 → 3 → 5.3 → 12 → 12 h at 2.6 s a stop. Easing runs in log(hours), so the drums roll evenly. Reduced motion jumps straight to each state.
- **Board:** HOURS / DAY · OWNED $ / M (red) · CLOUD GPU $0.97 · FRONTIER ~$12 · WRAPPED ≤$45, with the verdict as the note. A local rule (`VM_CSS` in `build.mjs`) gives the shared `.board` five equal columns above 700 px and shrinks the plate corners on phones. `_anatomy/` is untouched.
- **Build:** `node build.mjs` writes `vending.html`, `vending.fragment.html`, `vending.svg` and `vending-1..4.svg`. The build prints the projection scale; the smallest face text, 5.4 units, clears the token meter's 3.6-unit floor at this scale (5.25).
- **Checks:** `lines.mjs` reports 0 floating ends 3 runs in a row at the key state, 0 at states 1–3 and 0 under reduced motion. Also captured: desktop at 1200, phone at 390 (no sideways scroll), embed at 1280×720 (`?embed=1&state=2&still=1`), a tour over time, 4× close-ups of the dial and drum window, and the static `vending-1.svg`. The console is clean throughout.
- **Size:** Figure, about 75 parts (bench, crank box with dial, crank and shaft, cabinet, crown and marquee, 8 packs and a shelf behind glass, 4 slot modules with coin slots, return buttons and price plates, drum counter, lock, hinges, vents, serial plate). No shader.
