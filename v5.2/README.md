# V5.2 · An AI Manifesto — the talk, for the AI natives

Two editions from one source, both 25 minutes plus 5 minutes of Q&A, 22 slides each:

- **5.2G** (`g/`) — government. US-built models only, the end of the $1 era, the RFP told from the side of the people writing it (solicitation number, budget and evaluation plan removed at the gate; source selection plans and technical evaluation plans are source selection information under FAR 2.101), and the October 31 deadline.
- **5.2C** (`c/`) — commercial. Altman's Q1 budget quote, Ramp spend benchmarks, the best open model beating last spring's frontier, Cisco/Salesforce/Futurum proof.

What changed from 5.1, after the run-of-show analysis:

- **Timing.** Slide minutes sum to 25 and the presenter clock reads the total from them (it was hard-coded to 30:00). The notes run about 22 minutes at a natural pace, which leaves room to ad-lib.
- **Order.** The team ($1.3M month), contract and two-numbers slides are folded into the meter, side-door and Monday slides. The cost-of-owning slide now comes before the RFP, so the RFP leads into selling up the chain.
- **Language.** Notes and titles are rewritten in plain spoken English: full sentences, no em-dashes, no stage directions, and at most one contrast per slide. Dates are absolute ("read on October 18", "came out on October 12").
- **Follow-up.** The end slide has a QR code to the capacity ask (capacity-ask.vercel.app, repo `bradleynbaker/capacity-ask`).

`node build.mjs [G|C]` builds the variant figures (ladder, spikes, tubes and now rfp; each is line-checked), then both decks. Keys are the same as V5: → next, N notes, O ghost deck, P presenter, 1/2 hands on the vote.
